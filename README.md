# CityOS

### Smarter City. Seamless Life.

CityOS is a smart-city platform that I built with Pragun as a competition project. The idea was to explore how AI, GIS, GPS, live environmental data and citizen participation could be brought together to make everyday urban services more accessible.

**Live Demo:** https://unrivaled-taiyaki-413212.netlify.app/

## About the Project

The main idea behind CityOS was to connect different aspects of city life through one platform instead of treating every service as a separate system.

The project focuses on four main areas:

* Energy
* Governance
* Mobility
* Sustainability

## Features

### AI Electricity Bill Analyser

The Bill Analyser is designed to help users understand their electricity bills and get useful information from their consumption data.

It includes:

* Cost breakdown
* Consumption patterns
* Usage insights
* Peak usage analysis
* Savings recommendations

The basic workflow is:

State selection → DISCOM selection → Bill upload → AI analysis → Insights

### Civic Grievance & AI Hub

The Grievance Hub provides a structured way for citizens to report public and civic issues.

It includes:

* Complaint creation
* GPS location capture
* Location verification
* Ticket tracking
* Complaint download
* Official email generation

The aim is to make civic reporting more structured and easier to track.

### AirRoute

AirRoute is a route-planning concept that takes air quality into account.

It focuses on finding cleaner-air routes for walking and cycling using environmental and AQI data.

### ECO3D Map

ECO3D is the GIS and digital-twin component of CityOS.

It is designed around a 3D representation of an urban area and allows citizens to participate in verified urban tasks.

The concept includes:

* GIS-based 3D mapping
* Spatially accurate urban data
* Digital street layers
* Citizen task tracking
* Photo verification
* Reward points

The project uses Sadiq Nagar as the area for the digital-twin concept.

### CarbonQuest

CarbonQuest is a sustainability-focused part of CityOS that turns sustainable actions into a points-based system.

The concept includes activities such as walking, planting and clean-up actions, along with GPS-based participation, route tracking, impact scoring and verification.

The individual modules were designed to work as parts of one larger system.

                         CityOS

        |                 |                     |
        ------------------|----------------------

                    Sustainability

The overall idea is to connect citizens, data and AI through one platform.

## Design

For the interface, we wanted CityOS to feel more like a futuristic city control system than a traditional civic-services website.

The design follows a dark HUD-style interface with interactive elements and a futuristic visual approach.

## Why We Built It

Cities already produce a large amount of information and data. We wanted to explore how that information could be made more useful and easier for citizens to interact with.

CityOS was our attempt at bringing these different areas together into one platform.
