CityOS
Citizen Utility & AI Grievance Hub
CityOS is a smart-city web platform that brings citizen utilities, civic services, grievance management, and environmental intelligence into one interface. We built it to see how AI and better design could make city services feel less like paperwork and more like actually getting things done.

Live demo:
https://unrivaled-taiyaki-413212.netlify.app/

What it does
Bill Analyzer — helps people understand and break down their utility bills.

Grievance Hub — a place for citizens to raise and manage civic complaints.

AirRoute — a smart-city idea that combines route info with environmental data.

ECO3D / CarbonQuest — sustainability tools that help people understand their impact and make smarter choices.

Where AI comes in
A big part of CityOS is using AI to make civic information easier to deal with. The focus is on AI-assisted services, smarter information processing, environmental awareness, civic problem-solving, and interfaces that don’t feel like a headache.

Design
The look is dark HUD / smart-city control center. We wanted it to feel like you’re using a futuristic city operating system, not a standard government website.

Achievement
We built CityOS for a competition and took 1st place. The team got trophies too.
